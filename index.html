About me

I started out as a Zoology graduate who got curious about patterns, and ended up in data. Since 2021 I've grown at Hugo Technologies from Data Annotation Specialist to Senior Data Analyst, AI Operations, building the KPI dashboards, automations and data-quality systems that keep AI teams productive.

Outside of work I build end-to-end ML projects: I scrape the data, clean it, model it and deploy it as a Streamlit app you can play with.

Away from the screen, I love meeting new people, travelling to new places, and catching up on anime.
